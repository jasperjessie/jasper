Looking at the enormous interest generated by this model, we have certain insights and recommendations on how to make the most of ChatGPT and how to navigate through the shortcomings of this model.

## Some major limitations of Chat GPT

### Chat GPT often suffers from hallucinations

This is a nicer-sounding AI term that simply means that sometimes, the ChatGPT AI can present information that is incorrect and even express it confidently with falsely created facts and citations (Bowman 2022 and Simonite 2018).

Read [this](https://oxford-review.com/chatgpt-making-up-references/) to see how ChatGPT is making up academic references.

Data sources aren't real-time: OpenAI, the creator of ChatGPT, specifically states that one of its limitations is "limited knowledge of the world and events after 2021" ([Chat GPT help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6783457-chatgpt-general-faq), point 4). Its specific sources came from various sources including books, Wikipedia and other various web texts ([Thompson 2022](https://lifearchitect.ai/chatgpt/)). It has a huge but limited data set and cannot currently (as of January 2023) access the internet.

### Chat GPT is biased

The answers given by ChatGPT may not be as neutral as many users might expect. OpenAI's CEO, Sam Altman, admitted that ChatGPT has ["shortcomings around bias"](https://twitter.com/sama/status/1620927983627427840?s=20&t=1YV4bYfiTcj7onWo4OWFOg).

Timnit Gebru, the founder of DAIR, argues that simply using large amounts of text from the internet to train AI models doesn't ensure diversity. Instead, this text often reflects the views of those who remain online and may include sexist, [racist](https://in.mashable.com/tech/47980/chatgpt-new-ai-technology-old-racism-and-bias), ableist and other biased content. This can result in biased outputs from the AI models.

However, in order to make ChatGPT less violent, sexist, and racist, multiple guardrails have been put into place by OpenAI.

Have a look at [this](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/chatgpt-large-language-model-bias-60-minutes-2023-03-05/) article with the video on Timnit Gebru's interview on the same.

Having said all that, Chat GPT is unbelievably powerful and it is revolutionising the world, especially in education.

### Create Rubrics

Rubrics are powerful tools to help students prepare assignments. Chat GPT can aid in the development of course activity rubrics.

In the example below, I have included my specific requirements as part of the prompt.

Example prompt: *"Create a rubric to assess a classroom presentation. Here is the structure - Criteria will occupy the first column, Scoring components (Excellent, Good and needs improvement) will occupy the second, third and fourth columns respectively."*

![ChatGPT-generated rubric table with Criteria, Excellent, Good, and Needs Improvement columns](/blog/chatgpt-rubric.jpg)

### Create Assessments

Chat GPT can help streamline the process of creating assessments by quickly generating different exam questions based on your content.

Below are example prompts you can try feeding to Chat GPT.

#### Example prompts:

- **Write open-ended questions:** "Give me 10 question prompts to assess students' understanding of animal adaptations."
- **Write multiple-choice questions:** "Provide 10 multiple-choice questions about the period of Reconstruction."
- **Create questions based on a video transcript:** Copy YouTube (or other video) transcript and paste into ChatGPT. Ask to "Generate 5 multiple choice/fill-in-the-blank/open-ended questions from this transcript."
- **Generate Individual Education Plan (IEP) goals:** "Based on the following student abilities [insert abilities here], give me 5 specific and measurable goals for the student to work towards."
- **Create a study guide:** "Give me a study guide for an Indian Law student preparing for a test on the branches of Indian government."
- **Generate a set of practice problems:** "Create a set of practice problems for Engineering Mathematics students learning [insert topic name]."

#### Generate project ideas:

Chat GPT can help you brainstorm new ideas for student-led projects.

![ChatGPT prompt and response: student project ideas for demonstrating knowledge on Artificial Intelligence, including chatbot development, image classification, and speech recognition](/blog/chatgpt-project-ideas.jpg)

A simple prompt can be:

> *"What are student project ideas for demonstrating knowledge on Artificial Intelligence?"*

### Providing instructional assistance / Essay Grading

Chat GPT can serve as your perfect teaching assistant. It can help by providing quality feedback and instruction resources, allowing you to focus on adding your own ideas to the instruction/feedback.

For example, it can help grade written assignments automatically by training the model on a dataset of graded essays. Remember that the model works better by pushing it to do thorough work and adding follow-up instructions to make it more specific, detailed and accurate.

Example prompt: *Provide grading feedback on "\<essay\>"*

Follow-up prompt: *Come up with specifics.*

### Key points to consider

**Test your assignment prompts:** If you are concerned about whether students will use Chat GPT to answer assignments, then use Chat GPT to test your assignment questions before giving them out to students. Submit an assignment to Chat GPT to see how quickly and easily it provides an answer. Also, observe the quality of the solution that comes back from Chat GPT. Finally, modify your assignment and retest it until AI is no longer an easy tool to complete it.

**Check an essay/assignment submission to see if AI wrote it:** You can directly ask Chat GPT if a student wrote the essay or not — *"Was this written by Artificial Intelligence? '\<essay\>'"*

To conclude, Chat GPT could be better and it is vital that critical thinking should always be used in order to ensure that the results presented are logical, truthful and appropriate.
